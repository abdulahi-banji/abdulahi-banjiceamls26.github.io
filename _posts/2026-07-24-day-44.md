what_i_learned: |
  Today was our last day practicing in the lab before the final presentation. We started the day by doing another mock presentation, which went really well. Our team has changed the way we present, so instead of one person speaking for an entire slide, we transition between speakers throughout the presentation. Because of that, we've been putting extra effort into remembering who speaks next and making our transitions as smooth as possible.

  We also made a few final adjustments to our slides, including changing image sizes, updating fonts, and refining some of our explanations. With the presentation right around the corner, I've been spending time memorizing important concepts and making sure I understand not only my section but also how it connects to the rest of our project.

  One thing I learned today is that good presentations aren't just about memorizing lines—they're about understanding the material well enough to explain it confidently.
