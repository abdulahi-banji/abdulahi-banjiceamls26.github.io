what_i_learned: |
  There is no symposium today, but I wanted to take this opportunity to reflect on my experience throughout the SAIRI program. Coming into SAIRI, I honestly did not know what to expect. All I knew was that it was an internship and that I was getting a stipend. I had no idea that I would walk away with so much more than technical skills and research experience.

  Throughout this program, I learned so much about computer vision, object detection, robotics, edge AI, and conducting research. More importantly, I learned how to work effectively on a team, communicate technical concepts, present my work confidently, and network with others. I learned that growth does not only happen when writing code or training models. It also happens during conversations, presentations, failures, and late nights spent solving problems with your teammates.

  Beyond the technical experience, I made friendships that I know will last beyond this summer. From the long hours spent in the lab to hanging out outside of work(movies and restaurant), the people in this program made the experience even more meaningful.

reflection: |
  It feels difficult to put into words how grateful I am for this opportunity. Over the past several weeks, I have challenged myself in ways I did not expect. There were moments when our robot arm would not work, managing time efficiently felt difficult, and when I had to step outside of my comfort zone during presentations and networking events. Looking back now, I would not change any of it.

  One thing I have learned this summer is that hard work truly pays off. participating in my first hackathon and winning second place, presenting our final research project well, every experience contributed to my growth.

  As I pack my bags and prepare for what comes next, I find myself reminiscing about the beginning of the program. It feels like yesterday that we were introducing ourselves and trying to figure out what our projects would become. Now, we are leaving with completed research projects, new friendships, and memories that I know I will carry with me for a very long time.

  Thank you, Dr. Mack,  Michael, and  Elijah, for investing your time, energy, and wisdom into all of us. Thank you to my mentors, amazing teammates, and the entire SAIRI cohort for creating an environment where we could learn, grow, and challenge ourselves and have fun.

  Thank you, SAIRI. This may be the end of the internship, but it is only the beginning of everything that comes next.
